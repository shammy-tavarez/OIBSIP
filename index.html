The Missile Man of India

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam is known as the "Missile Man of India" because of his pivotal role in developing India's missile technology, particularly the Agni and Prithvi missiles, and his leadership of the Integrated Guided Missile Development Program (IGMDP).

  • Integrated Guided Missile Development Program (IGMDP): Kalam led the IGMDP, a crucial program that developed India's missile technology, including the Agni and Prithvi missiles.
  • Agni and Prithvi Missiles: These missiles were instrumental in enhancing India's defense capabilities and establishing the country as a force to be reckoned with in missile technology.
  • efense Research and Development Organisation (DRDO): Kalam worked with DRDO, contributing significantly to India's defense technology.
  • Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO): Kalam also worked with ISRO, contributing to India's space program.

Timeline of Achievements

  • 1931 - Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu
  • 1954 - Graduated in Physics from St. Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli
  • 1960 - Joined DRDO as chief scientist
  • 1969 - Transferred to ISRO as project director of India's first Satellite Launch Vehicle
  • 1982 - Became Director of DRDO and oversaw multiple military projects
  • 1998 - Played key role in Pokhran-II nuclear tests
  • 2002 - Elected 11th President of India
  • 2012 - Launched 'What Can I Give Movement' for social development
  • 2015 - Passed away while delivering lecture at IIM Shillong
